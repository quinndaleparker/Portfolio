Self-taught, and deliberately so. I learned to build because the organizations around me needed tools that didn't exist yet, and I've been shipping them ever since under my own practice, QuinnPark.

My work sits in two halves that keep feeding each other. On the front end I build with React, Next.js, Vue and TypeScript; on the back end, Node and Python. The other half is the data itself — designing how a clinic captures a behavior, where it lands, and how a director sees it three months later without opening a spreadsheet.

That combination is most of what I do: an intake form that a technician can finish in ninety seconds, wired to a warehouse that a supervisor can query, surfaced in a dashboard that reads clearly in a parent meeting. I care about the whole line, not one end of it.
